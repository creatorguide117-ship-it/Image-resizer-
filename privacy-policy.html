Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 July 2026

This policy explains what data ImageResizer.tech ("the site", "we") does and doesn't handle when you use it.

Your images never leave your device

All image processing on this site — resizing, compression, format conversion — is performed locally in your web browser using JavaScript and the Canvas API. Your images are never uploaded to, stored on, or seen by our servers or any third party via this site's tools. You can verify this yourself: load the page, disconnect from the internet, and the tool keeps working.

Information we do not collect

We do not require accounts, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or the contents of any file you process. We have no database of user files, because no files reach us.

Cookies and advertising

To keep the tool free, the site may display advertising served by third parties such as Google AdSense. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies is described in Google's advertising policies, and you can manage personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. We do not control the cookies set by advertising partners.

Analytics

The site may use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics (such as page-view counts) to understand which tools are useful. Any analytics used do not involve the images you process.

Third-party resources

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and the JSZip library from the cdnjs content delivery network. These providers may log standard technical request data (such as IP address) when your browser fetches those resources, as described in their own privacy policies.

Children

The site is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@imageresizer.tech.
