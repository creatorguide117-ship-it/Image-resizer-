Passport Photo Size by Country — Full Reference

Passport and visa photo dimensions vary by country, and getting them wrong is one of the most common reasons applications bounce back. Here are the standard sizes for major countries, in cm and the pixel equivalent at 300 DPI (the usual print/scan standard).

CountryPhysical sizePixels @ 300 DPI
India3.5 × 4.5 cm413 × 531 px
United Kingdom3.5 × 4.5 cm413 × 531 px
European Union / Schengen3.5 × 4.5 cm413 × 531 px
United States2 × 2 in (5.1 × 5.1 cm)600 × 600 px
Canada5.0 × 7.0 cm591 × 827 px
Australia3.5 × 4.5 cm413 × 531 px

These are the standard official dimensions, but requirements do change and some countries specify a head-size range within the frame too (for example, the US requires the head to be 1–1⅜ inches from chin to crown). Always check your country's current passport office guidance for the fine print — this table covers the size and format, not every composition rule.

Step by step

  1. Take or scan a photo with a plain light background, facing forward, neutral expression.
  2. Open the resizer and choose Print size (cm) mode.
  3. Enter the cm dimensions from the table for your country, DPI at 300.
  4. Most passport portals also cap the file size — commonly 20–50KB — so add that in the optional max-size box.
  5. Drop your photo. It's centre-cropped to the exact shape, so make sure your face is centred with even margin around it before resizing.
