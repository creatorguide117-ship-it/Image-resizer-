Before you write — quick answers

  • The output looks blurry. Very tight KB targets (10–20KB) on large photos always cost sharpness. Try a slightly larger target, or reduce the pixel dimensions first.
  • The KB cap wasn't reached in Pixels/Print mode. At a fixed dimension there's a floor to how small a file can get. Reduce the dimensions or raise the cap.
  • PNG files stay big. PNG is lossless, so quality search doesn't apply — switch the output format to JPEG or WebP for small targets.
